About the Chorale

Learn about the chorale's history and musical leadership.

1954–2026: A Brief History

The Ellwood City Area Civic Chorale was formed in 1954 by former students of the Ellwood City Lincoln High School choir who wanted to continue singing together. Approximately thirty singers from Pennsylvania and Ohio comprise the current chorale. The chorale presents two concert seasons each year: spring/summer and Christmas holiday. Programs typically include Broadway tunes, secular music, Gospel songs, and patriotic music. The group also performs for churches, retirement homes, community events, and special occasions.

Over the years, the Chorale has sung for regional arts festivals, community celebrations, holiday traditions, church events, retirement homes, and memorial services. The group continues to share music locally through a love of song.

Joyce Turner-Gindlesperger, Director

Joyce Turner-Gindlesperger joined the Chorale in 1980 after graduating from New Brighton High School. Her civic chorale journey began as an alto section singer, and she later served as accompanist and assistant director before being appointed director in 1993. Joyce has served in church music roles for many years and operates a piano teaching studio from her home. Her professional affiliations include the National Piano Teachers' Guild, the American Choral Directors Association, the American Guild of English Hand Bell Ringers, and the National Federation of Music Clubs.

Cindy R. Cotherman, Accompanist

Cindy R. Cotherman began piano lessons in third grade and has been playing ever since. She has accompanied and directed a wide range of musical theater productions, including work with Liberty Theatre, New Castle Playhouse, and the Strand Theater. Cindy is employed by Felician Sisters of North America and serves as rehearsal accompanist and orchestra member for local high school theater productions.
