Patrons

Patron support helps keep community choral music alive in Ellwood City.

Support the Chorale

The Ellwood City Area Civic Chorale pays for the majority of its operating costs through the generosity of its patrons. To support the chorale, send a contribution to: Ellwood City Area Civic Chorale, P.O. Box 885, Ellwood City, PA 16117.

Patrons may be recognized in concert programs and on this website. Confirm permission before publishing any new patron names online.
